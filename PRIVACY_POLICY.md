# Privacy Policy for Spewboard

**Last updated: November 2026**

## 1. Audio & Microphone Data
Spewboard uses your device microphone (RECORD_AUDIO) solely for real-time voice effects (VoiceFxProcessor) and local clip recording. Audio is processed locally on your device and routed through your audio output. We do not upload or store your voice chat audio on external servers.

## 2. Feedback & Diagnostics
When you voluntarily submit feedback through the in-app feedback button, the text you enter and basic app version details are sent to the developers solely to fix bugs and improve the app.

## 3. Third-Party Content
Any external audio files imported by the user remain stored locally on the user's device. Users are responsible for ensuring they have the right to use imported audio clips.
